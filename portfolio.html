Efficient LLM Fine-Tuning using LoRA & QLoRA (TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat)

Fine-tuned the TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat LLM for Text-to-SQL generation using PEFT techniques, including LoRA and QLoRA. Trained on approximately 3,000 high-quality SQL examples from the Hugging Face b-mc2/sql-create-context dataset using Google Colab NVIDIA T4 GPU. The model completed 3 training epochs, achieving 84.2% validation accuracy while significantly reducing GPU memory consumption and training costs through 4-bit quantization, low-rank adaptation, and efficient PEFT techniques.

n8n Cloud Automation

AI Lead Generation & Cold Outreach Automation

A fully automated prospecting pipeline that discovers businesses in any region, works out what they are likely struggling with, and prepares a personalized solution email for each one — ready to review in Gmail.

Platformn8n Cloud
Target RegionsUK, US & Custom
Lead StorageGoogle Sheets
OutputGmail Drafts

The Problem

Finding clients manually means hours of searching for businesses, copying their details, guessing what they need, and writing every outreach email from scratch. It is slow, repetitive, and hard to scale.

How It Works

  1. Choose a target Pick a region (UK, US, or anywhere) and a business category to search for.
  2. Discover companies on Google Maps The workflow searches Google Maps and collects every matching business in that area.
  3. Store everything in Google Sheets Company name, address, phone, website, and other details are saved to a clean, region-wise sheet.
  4. AI analyzes each company An AI agent reviews each business and identifies the problems and gaps it is most likely facing.
  5. Match problems to my services The AI maps each problem to the service that solves it and shapes a tailored solution.
  6. Draft the outreach email in Gmail A personalized email is written and saved as a draft, so every message is reviewed before it is sent.

Key Features

  • Region-based targeting — switch markets by changing a single input
  • Automatic data capture — no copy-pasting, no manual research
  • Personalized emails — each one speaks to that company's specific problem
  • Human-in-the-loop — drafts only, so nothing goes out unreviewed
  • Scalable — run it for hundreds of companies in the same time as one
  • Fully hands-off — runs end to end inside n8n Cloud
